## Hi, I'm Nico 👋

I started out writing code and grew into the person who connects the pieces: leading engineering teams, turning ideas into products people use, and putting AI to work where it actually saves time.

- 🧭 **Engineering lead** — architecture, team practices and reliable releases for a multi-tenant health-tech platform (patient monitoring, device telemetry, alerting).
- 🚀 **Product builder** — I take my own products from idea to production, end to end: design, backend, frontend and infra.
- 🤖 **AI & automation** — LLM agents, MCP servers and n8n workflows that replace manual work across teams.
- 🏠 **Maker at heart** — a self-hosted homelab, Home Assistant, Raspberry Pi and Arduino around the house.

## 🚧 What I'm building

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| ⏱️ **Time intelligence** | Automatic time tracking per project and task that compares real hours against the plan and flags when the pace is off. Self-hosted. |
| 💸 **Personal finance** | Household finance dashboard for Israel: pulls every bank and credit card into one place and uses Claude to categorize spending. |
